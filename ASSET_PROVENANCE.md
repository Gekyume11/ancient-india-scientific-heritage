# Presentation asset provenance

## ChatGPT-generated imagery

Six images were generated in the user’s logged-in ChatGPT session for this presentation, then saved locally. Original PNGs remain in `work/generated-originals/` in the source workspace. The presentation uses WebP copies at quality 88 to reduce download size; cropping and grayscale display are applied with CSS.

These images are conceptual editorial illustrations. They do not document a particular archaeological site, artifact, instrument or historical event. Relevant slide notes describe this distinction.

All six prompts requested monochrome fine-art photography, restrained museum-style composition, realistic tactile materials, subtle film grain, and no overlaid text, logos, borders or watermarks. Full prompts for astronomy, mathematics, medicine, metallurgy and yoga are recorded in `work/image-prompts.json`. The stepwell prompt requested a portrait architectural study of descending stone stairs and carved pillared galleries in Gujarat, with morning haze and no people.
